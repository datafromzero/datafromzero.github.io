After the advanced core you choose one of four tracks: Data Analyst, Data Engineer, Data Scientist or ML / AI Engineer.
A track is not more of the same. It works like a master's degree: **less coding, more mathematics and statistics, and a
great deal of knowledge and judgment.** Each track also teaches the specialist skills of its own career, which the core
only touches.

Think carefully before you choose. The track decides what you study next, and the four careers lean on very different skills.

Not sure yet? [Take the career quiz](/career-quiz/) again. It asks ten questions about how you like to work and suggests a
direction. Then come back and compare it with the chart below.

The scores are a guide drawn from the research behind this site, **not a measurement.** Real jobs differ from one company
to the next, and any role can lean toward another's skills. Use the chart to ask a question: which of these skills do I want
to be the strongest part of my working day?
