## Cite one lesson

Every lesson ends with a ready-made **Cite this lesson** line with the author, the year the lesson
was last reviewed, its title, the curriculum version and its address. A page cannot know when you
read it, so add your own access date.

## Cite the whole work

Use these entries to cite datafromzero as a whole. They are generated from the project's
`CITATION.cff` file, so they always match the current curriculum version. Add the date you
accessed the site if your style asks for it.
