datafromzero is open. Three licenses apply, depending on what you reuse.

| What | License |
|---|---|
| Content: lessons, reference pages, stories, career guides | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| Code: site code, components, scripts, code samples | [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit) |
| Datasets and their documentation | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

## What you can do

You may copy, share and adapt the material for any purpose, including commercial use, as long
as you:

- give appropriate credit (see the [cite](/cite/) page for the exact form),
- link to the license, and
- say if you made changes.

## Fonts

The site's fonts are self-hosted: Geist and Geist Mono (from the `@fontsource` packages) on every page, and Geist Serif, only on the
[completion page](/completion/), from the official `geist` package of Vercel (copyright 2023 Vercel, in collaboration with
basement.studio). All three are licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org). The text of the
license for Geist Serif is kept with the font file, in `public/fonts/geist-serif/OFL.txt` in the repository.

## Where the texts live

The two license files are in the root of the [project repository](https://github.com/datafromzero/datafromzero.github.io):
`LICENSE-CONTENT` for content and datasets, and `LICENSE` for code. Their full texts are shown below, read from those files.
