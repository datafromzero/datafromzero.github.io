Your message was not sent. This can happen when the message is empty or too long (the limit is 2000
characters), when several messages were sent in a short time, or when the service is busy.

Use your browser's Back button to return to the lesson. Your text is usually still in the box. If it keeps
failing, you can
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/datafromzero/datafromzero.github.io/issues/new?template=page-report.yml)
yourself.
