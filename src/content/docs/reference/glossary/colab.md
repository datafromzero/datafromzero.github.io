Google Colab, short for Colaboratory, is a hosted Jupyter notebook service: Google runs the computer, and you work in your browser. Its FAQ says Colab requires no setup to use and is free of charge. You sign in with a Google account, and your notebook is saved in your Google Drive. Its free resources are not guaranteed and its limits change, so the course never depends on a number from it. See [notebook](/reference/glossary/notebook/) for what a notebook is.
