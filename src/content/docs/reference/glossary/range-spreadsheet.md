A range is a block of cells in a worksheet, written as its first cell, a colon and its last cell: `F2:F8` is the seven cells from F2 down to F8. Microsoft Support says that a reference identifies a cell or a range of cells on a worksheet. A function such as `SUM(F2:F8)` reads every cell in the range. Google Sheets writes ranges the same way.

## Where you meet it

- [*Your first formulas*](/excel/first-formulas/) (E2): SUM, COUNT and AVERAGE read a range.
- [*Tables, sorting and filtering*](/excel/tables-sort-filter/) (E5): a Table is a range that Excel treats as one object.
- [*Conditional totals with SUMIFS and COUNTIFS*](/excel/conditional-totals/) (E7): SUMIFS and COUNTIFS name a sum range and ranges to test.
