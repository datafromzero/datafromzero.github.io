term: Confidence interval
short: A range around an estimate, such as a share or an average, that shows how much it could move if you sampled again.
aliases: [CI, interval, confidence intervals]
reviewed: 2026-11-27
---

A confidence interval is an estimate plus and minus a margin. NIST describes it as a range of values that is likely to contain the population value you care about. At a 95 percent level, if you repeated the sampling many times, about 95 percent of the intervals built this way would contain the true value. A wide interval says you do not know much yet; a narrow one says the estimate is steady.

## Where you meet it

- [*Confidence intervals*](/core/confidence-intervals/) (C7): build the interval for a share and for a mean.
- [*Comparing two groups: tests and effect sizes*](/core/comparing-groups/) (C9): report an interval beside the p-value.
