In an observational study nobody assigns the groups: the data arise on their own and you only watch. The open course text Concepts in Statistics puts it as researchers collecting data and looking for links without manipulating the explanatory variable. It can show an association, two things that move together, but not by itself a causal connection. An experiment, in which you assign the treatment at random, is what lets you credit a difference to the treatment. All of cafe-sales is observational: the payment method of an order was there before anyone looked.
