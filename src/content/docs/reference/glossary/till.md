A till is a cash register: the machine and drawer where a customer pays and the sale is recorded. In the cafe-sales data, every order is one visit to the till. When a lesson says "the till system," it means the software that keeps those orders, and its report is the control total your own numbers must match: it reports 1,800 orders, 1,799 of them completed.

## Where you meet it

- [*Rows, columns and tidy layout*](/excel/grid-and-tidy-layout/) (E1): the order list of the till system is the first table you meet.
- [*A cleaning workflow you can defend*](/excel/cleaning-workflow/) (E13): the till system's report is the control total for a cleaning.
