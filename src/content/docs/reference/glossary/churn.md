Churn is when customers leave, stop paying or go quiet. Because "gone" can mean different things, every lesson that uses the word defines it first. In the Data Analyst track, logo churn is the share of customers who left, and revenue churn is the share of the recurring revenue that left; which one you need depends on the decision. In the Data Scientist track, a person has churned when there is no visit and no expense in days 8 to 30 after signing up. These lessons use a made-up budgeting app, not the cafes.
